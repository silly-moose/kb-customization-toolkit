Use this when a customer contacts us because they can't sign in to their account.

Before you start:

  • Open the customer's account in the admin tool.
  • Ask the customer to have the device they sign in with nearby.

Action: Confirm who you're talking to

Ask for the email address on the account and one security detail, such as the billing postcode.

Confirm: I verified the customer's identity.

Decision: What happens when they try to sign in?

  • It says the password is wrong → Reset the password
  • The sign-in code never arrives → Check the email address
  • It says the account is locked → Locked account

Action: Reset the password

Send a password reset email from the admin tool and stay on the line while they open it. The link expires after 30 minutes.

Go to: Confirm they can sign in

Decision: Check the email address

Read back the email address on the account.

  • It's correct → Resend the code
  • It's out of date → Update the email address

Action: Resend the code

Ask them to check their spam folder, then resend the code from the admin tool.

Go to: Confirm they can sign in

Action: Update the email address

Changing the email address on an account needs a second check.

Escalate: Hand off to a team lead to verify and make the change. Don't change it yourself.

End: Handed off to a team lead to update the email address.

Judgement: Locked account

Accounts lock after five failed attempts or when we see unusual activity. Ask whether they recognize the recent sign-in attempts.

Escalate: If they don't recognize the attempts, treat it as possible fraud and transfer them to the security team.

End: Account unlocked, or the customer was transferred to the security team.

Action: Confirm they can sign in

Stay with them until they're signed in, then tell them how to reset their password themselves next time.

End: The customer is signed in.
